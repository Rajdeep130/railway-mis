How it works

Roles. A site engineer enters daily progress. The project manager approves it. The billing executive bills only approved work. Nobody approves their own entry.

Emails. Tender reminders go out 7, 3, 1 and 0 days before the due date, and every day once overdue. A log stops any email being sent twice.

Numbers. Progress % is executed quantity over planned quantity from the BOQ. Net payable is the certified amount less deductions. A bill is overdue when it is unpaid more than 30 days after certification.

Dashboard. Apps Script recalculates report tabs every 15 minutes, and Looker Studio reads them.

What it is not

All data is demo data, and everything runs on free plans.

It is not production-hardened. The audit trail is the log tabs only, and roles rely on the app sign-in.

I built it with AI assistance, and I can explain every formula, script and design choice.
